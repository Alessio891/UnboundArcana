# Unbound Arcana - Lore and Identity

## Philosophy

Lore exists to support gameplay.

The hierarchy is:

1. Mechanics
2. Systemic identity
3. Lore

The game must remain enjoyable even if the player ignores the story.

The world exists to explain why the mechanics exist.

---

# The Player

The player is an arcane researcher.

They investigate magic as a system.

They are not:

* A hero fulfilling a prophecy.
* A wizard collecting ancient spells.
* A warrior becoming stronger through equipment.

They are an experimenter.

---

# The Tower

The Tower is not the main point of the game.

The Tower exists to support the spell creation fantasy.

It is:

* A laboratory
* A containment system
* An archive of magical experiments

---

# Origin of the Tower

Ancient researchers discovered a source of magic beyond normal understanding.

They attempted to study it.

Their experiments altered reality itself.

The Tower emerged as a consequence of those experiments.

It became a place where:

* Experiments were stored.
* Reality became unstable.
* Magical principles were discovered.
* Previous researchers left incomplete knowledge.

---

# Research Philosophy

The player is not searching for lost spells.

They are reconstructing magical principles.

The Tower contains fragments of understanding.

The player combines these fragments into new magical systems.

---

# Research Branches

The Tower contains different areas of magical investigation.

Examples:

## Fragmentation

Magic splitting beyond intended boundaries.

Themes:

* Duplication
* Division
* Multiple outcomes

---

## Thermal Conversion

Magic interacting with heat and energy.

Themes:

* Combustion
* Transformation
* Propagation

---

## Spatial Manipulation

Magic affecting distance and position.

Themes:

* Teleportation
* Compression
* Distortion

---

## Temporal Distortion

Magic interacting with time.

Themes:

* Repetition
* Delay
* Acceleration

---

# Arcane Instability

Instability represents the difference between controlled research and dangerous experimentation.

The most powerful discoveries often approach the limits of magical control.

Instability should represent:

* Possibility
* Risk
* Complexity

It should not simply represent failure.

A researcher does not avoid instability completely.

They learn how to use it.

---

# The Tower as a Living Archive

The Tower is not a normal dungeon.

It represents accumulated magical experimentation.

Each run explores different consequences of previous research.

Rooms, enemies, and discoveries are manifestations of magical theories colliding.

---

# Core Theme

The central question of Unbound Arcana:

> How far can magic be pushed before understanding is lost?

The player is not searching for the strongest spell.

They are searching for the limits of what a spell can become.
